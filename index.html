About Rhilea

I’m Rhilea, a passionate and versatile dancer with a love for movement, storytelling and live performance. I bring energy, creativity and commitment to every stage, event and studio.

From performances at parties and functions to themed costume appearances and dance workshops for students, I love creating memorable experiences and sharing the joy of dance.

Available for bookings across Melbourne and surrounding areas.
